# lists (`cad-runtime-key.sh --backend`, `cad-runtime-scope.sh --backend`), so a change to the app alone runs the

# and tauri-build inputs. The files outside src-tauri/ below are ones the crate compiles in, its tests read, or
# tauri-build copies. scripts/ci/test/cad-backend-inputs.sh walks the crate for include_str!, include_bytes!,
# CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR paths, and the bundle in tauri.conf.json, and fails on any input outside both lists.
